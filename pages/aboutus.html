About Aurelia Grand Hotel

Our Story

Our history of excellence dates back long enough and is evident in each of our details that include our beautiful spacing and the warm hospitality that made us who we are. Over the years, we have been able to stick to the principles of giving timeless sophistication, and we are ever changing to reach the expectations of the ultimate standards of modern luxuries to make each stay an unforgettable experience.

Luxury Experience

Dining

A combination of sophisticated cuisines and sophisticated atmosphere is featured in our cuisine where we combine local influences with a global cuisine blended by highly qualified chefs. Each food is carefully made with high quality dishes and presented in exquisite environment which converts the ordinary food into an unforgettable experience.

Spa

Our spa is a peaceful haven meant to bring back the balance and revive the senses. Through professional care services, serene decor, and customized wellness programmes, the guests will be able to relax in an environment of complete tranquility and walk out feeling refreshed, rejuvenated and rejuvenated. Massages, energy-renewing treatments, all the details are carefully selected to enable a complete escape of the life routine.

Rooms

We have beautifully furnished rooms incorporating comfort and sophistication, in order to offer you a relaxing experience at the end of a day of exploration or business. With comfortable furniture, contemporary comforts, and sophisticated details, every room guarantees privacy, comfort, and the extra luxurious experience.

Event's Place

Our events place presents a flexible and well-decorated facility that is ideal in the celebration, conferences, and special events. With state-of-the-art facilities and a dedicated events team, we manage to make every event run smoothly bringing memorable experiences to both hosts and guests.

Indulge in refined comfort, timeless style, and exceptional service. Your unforgettable luxury escape begins here.
