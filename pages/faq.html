FAQ'S & POLICIES

FAQ Accordion
Do you provide airport transfer services?

Yes, we offer individual airport services to make your arrival a joyous luxurious experience. The visitors can select luxurious sedan or spacious SUVs at their own discretion. It is advisable to make advance reservations to ensure it is available.

Is there Wi-Fi everywhere?

There is complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi in every guest room and common place. Connection is safe and appropriate to recreational browsing and business requirements. Our technical department keeps you connected always during your stay.

Do you offer meeting or events space?

Yes, we have high-tech meetings and conference rooms in which corporate meetings and social gatherings can take place. All the venues have contemporary audiovisual facilities and flexible seating selections. We have an events team that helps in the planning so that it can be a smooth and memorable event.

What is your cancellation policy?

Guests can cancel their booking without penalty as long as they do it within the specified cancellation date before arrival. Any cancellations made after this are subject to any charges that might be incurred according to the conditions of the booking. We suggest that you check your reservation information or call our front desk in case of any particular query about your booking.
